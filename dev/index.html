RigorousUlam2D.jl

The computations of Section 6 ("Examples") and Theorem E of Pacifico, Bioni Liberalquino, Nisoli, Stationary densities of random maps with additive BV noise: existence, regularity and certified Ulam approximation: for a map of the two-torus with additive uniform noise, a computed stationary density on a uniform grid and a certified upper bound on its L¹ distance from the stationary density.

RigorousUlam2D.RigorousUlam2D — Module

The certified Ulam approximation of the stationary density of a map of the two-torus with additive uniform noise, and the certified L¹ distance between the computed density and the stationary one: the computations of Section 6 ("Examples") of Pacifico, Bioni Liberalquino, Nisoli, "Stationary densities of random maps with additive BV noise: existence, regularity and certified Ulam approximation".

The stages, each in its own file:

  • grid.jl: the uniform dyadic grids and the two orders of their cells;
  • transition_graph.jl: the enclosure L_lo ≤ L ≤ L_lo + U of the Ulam matrix of a map (UlamTree, refine!, ulam_matrices, fibred_area);
  • maps.jl: the maps of the paper's examples;
  • noisy_operator.jl: the noisy Ulam operator P_h = π N_ξ π L π, matrix-free, with its ℓ¹ rounding model;
  • norms_of_powers.jl: C_j ≥ ‖P_h^j|_{V_h}‖₁ by iterating every column;
  • l1_error.jl: the computed density, its residuals, the coarse-fine recursion and the L¹ bounds;
  • experiments.jl, stages.jl: the named stages the drivers in bench/ are thin over (run_blocks, run_stationary_density, run_column_sweep, run_transfer), and the names of their files;
  • archive.jl, deposit.jl: the archival copies and the deposit of the data.
source

The stages, in the order a run goes through them, are listed in the API reference; the drivers that chain them and write the records are in bench/ and paper/ of the repository, and the worked examples, one per map, are the pages that follow (docs/literate/).
